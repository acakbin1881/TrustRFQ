<objective>
Close the registry: admin-tunable spam pricing, and the security surface REG-03 names — re-init
guard, auth on every mutating call, events on every state transition, and TTL behavior.

Purpose: everything up to here proves the registry does what it should when used correctly. This
plan proves it refuses what it should. The two knobs are what make a stake a *tunable* spam price
rather than a number frozen at deploy time, and the rejection tests are what stop a later refactor
from silently deleting a check.

Output: `set_costs`, `set_max_makers_per_token`, and a test module that fails if any auth check,
bound, guard, or event is removed.
</objective>

3. **The `upgrade` omission is a planner decision.** `contracts/rfq_swap/src/lib.rs` ships an
